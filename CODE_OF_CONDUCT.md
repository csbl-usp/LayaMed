# Code of conduct

LayaMed follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the
community. Harassment, insults and personal or political attacks are not accepted.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in
public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers of the
[CSBL-USP organization](https://github.com/csbl-usp). Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers
may remove comments or contributions and block participants who do not follow this code.
