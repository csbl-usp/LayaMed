Highlights

      
Small model, careful edges

      
An encoder that reads the whole case at once is fast enough to follow typing. The rest of the design
        is about not saying more than it knows.

      

        

          
Live while typing
One analysis takes about 50 ms on a laptop GPU, so the bars update on every pause.

        

          
Out-of-scope guard
A recipe or a news story gets no probability instead of a forced diagnosis.

        

          
Calibrated as you write
Probabilities stay moderate while the case is incomplete and grow with exam and labs.

        

          
Runs locally
A plain HTML page and a FastAPI server on your machine. No CDN; nothing leaves it.
